Birds of Bengaluru

A 200-species identification course focused on field marks, habitat, behaviour, comparison, photography and sound. Built for beginner-to-intermediate birders in Bengaluru and nearby habitats.

Media & accuracy policy
Photos are retrieved from Wikimedia Commons search results at viewing time rather than copied into this repository. Each card also links to Cornell eBird/Macaulay Library for regional photographs and sound recordings, and to Xeno-canto for field recordings. Always check the recording/photo page for its attribution and licence before reusing media outside this course. Species names are aligned to the Bengaluru regional checklist used for this course; local occurrence can be seasonal or habitat-specific.

Important: this repository intentionally links to authoritative media rather than redistributing third-party recordings. The sound buttons open the relevant recording/search collection so learners can hear the species and inspect attribution.
